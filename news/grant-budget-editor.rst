**Added:**

* The a_grant helper now gives each new grant a budget with one period per grant year, sharing the amount between them by their length, or taking an amount for each year with --annual-amounts.
* The u_grant helper can now change a grant's budget: --edit-budget opens the budget periods in your text editor, checks them when you save and lets you fix any problem; --budget-file reads them from a YAML file, for the helper GUI; and --annual-amounts makes one period per grant year with the amounts given.
