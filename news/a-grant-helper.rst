**Added:**

* Add an ``a_grant`` helper. With ``--from-proposal`` it seeds the grant from a funded proposal, copying its title, amount, currency, funder, call, dates (written as ISO dates even when the proposal used day, month and year), team, institution, person-months and scope, applies any overrides such as the actual ``--begin-date`` or awarded ``--amount``, links the grant to the proposal and marks the proposal accepted. Team members without an institution are looked up in people and contacts, and anything still missing is reported all at once before the grant is written.
